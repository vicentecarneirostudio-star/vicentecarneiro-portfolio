RESUME CV

Senior Product Designer and AI builder with over 10 years of experience in product design and branding for digital products.

I currently work at North, embedded with CaixaBank designing complex fintech experiences for InOne — a digital banking app used by over 500K users. AI is a core part of how I work, and I continuously explore new tools to optimize the design process, from research and ideation to decision-making. I cover the full design process, from discovery and user journeys to wireframes, design systems, and documentation, with a focus on BNPL and payment flows. I'm also involved in implementing W3C accessibility standards.

Previously, I led the UX/UI team at DDB Spain for ALDI, and had the opportunity to collaborate with major brands such as CaixaBank, Beko, Fundación La Caixa, Haier, FGC, and the City of Barcelona. Working closely with their marketing teams, we achieved outstanding results in enhancing their digital products.
