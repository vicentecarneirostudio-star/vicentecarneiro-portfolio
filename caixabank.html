The Challenge

CaixaBank commissioned Manifiesto to develop a UX/UI project to support the launch of a new business line focused on solar panels and smart security systems. The main challenge was to build trust and create an emotional connection, making users feel that their home would be safe and worry-free, allowing them to focus their energy on what truly matters.

From a strategic perspective, the key objective was to drive users to click the “Learn More” button, turning curiosity into action and boosting conversions through clear messaging and an intuitive digital experience.

We created two landing pages built on different strategies to test user response and optimize conversion performance.

/ The Strategy Behind Every Click

To drive user engagement and increase conversion rates, we designed and implemented a dual-website A/B testing strategy. Each version presented different UX approaches, visual hierarchies, and messaging tones to identify what resonated most with users.

Strategy A
The Emotional Approach

In this version, we focused on creating an emotional connection with users by appealing to feelings of safety, comfort, and trust. The visual direction featured a young couple aged between 35 and 40 — representing CaixaBank’s target audience for new home and energy products. The color palette was built around calm blue tones, symbolizing security and reliability, while soft imagery reinforced the sense of a balanced, worry-free home. The “Learn more” CTA was strategically placed only at the beginning and end of the landing page to maintain a smooth reading flow and encourage users to engage emotionally before taking action. This approach aimed to increase curiosity and trust, leading users to explore more deeply and click with intention rather than impulse.

Strategy B
The Functional Approach

In this version, we adopted a more direct and functional strategy focused on clarity and accessibility. The visual identity shifted to a more mature audience, featuring models aged between 45 and 60 to reflect experience, confidence, and decision-making power. The dominant color palette moved from calm blues to vibrant greens, representing growth, energy efficiency, and sustainability — values closely tied to CaixaBank’s new solar and home security products. Unlike Strategy A, this version included multiple “Learn more” CTAs placed strategically across every section of the page. This allowed users to take action at any point of their browsing experience, significantly improving the click-through rate and overall engagement metrics. By combining accessibility, visual trust, and a clearer interaction flow, Strategy B encouraged users to act with confidence and intent.

Conclusions & Learnings

This project demonstrated how UX strategy and experimentation can directly impact business results. By testing two different landing page approaches, we identified the most effective combination of visual language, audience targeting, and CTA placement. The optimized version achieved a 30% increase in user clicks, proving that thoughtful UX decisions and continuous testing can significantly improve conversion performance and user engagement.

Strategy B delivered the strongest performance results

/ The Results That Matter

Our dual-website A/B testing strategy delivered clear insights. Version B, with its warmer visuals, green palette, and accessible CTAs, increased user interaction and clicks by over 30%. These results validated the importance of testing and tailoring UX decisions to real user behavior.
