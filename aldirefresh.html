ALDI is a leading global supermarket, with over 12,000 stores worldwide.

About ALDI

With a global turnover exceeding €112 billion in 2023, ALDI has become a major player in the food retail sector. In Spain alone, it has more than 480 stores and serves over 7 million households. Its mission is to provide affordable, everyday groceries without compromising on value or quality — making it a vital choice for millions of consumers.

The Challenge

ALDI tasked us with refreshing and strengthening its digital presence to better reflect the brand’s values of simplicity, quality, and trust — while also improving user interaction and guiding users toward meaningful engagement online.

The key challenge was to design a user experience that felt familiar and intuitive to a wide range of customers, from everyday grocery shoppers to value-seeking households, and to create a digital journey that encourages users to explore ALDI’s offerings more deeply and take action (such as discovering promotions, services, and store information).

We needed to strike the right balance between ALDI’s established brand identity and a more modern, responsive digital interface that enhances usability, supports accessibility standards, and aligns with user expectations in a competitive retail landscape.

/ Why change was necessary

The previous visual identity had become outdated and was no longer aligned with the brand’s evolving business goals. To stay competitive and relevant, we needed a fresher, more contemporary design language that better reflected current standards and strengthened user engagement.

The brand refresh

Overview

We designed and implemented a full digital ecosystem for ALDI across web, mobile, and app, ensuring a consistent and cohesive experience regardless of the platform customers used to interact with the brand. Through a unified design system and shared UX foundations, we aligned navigation, content, and visual language across all three touchpoints — allowing users to move seamlessly between browsing on web, shopping on mobile, and using the app for daily convenience.
