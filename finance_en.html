Introduction

The Challenge

The goal was to turn InOne into an app that is easy for customers to use, despite the complexity of its financial flows and operations. The main challenge was to make every journey feel clear and effortless, so people could handle complex tasks with confidence, without losing the trust that a banking product has to convey.

From a strategic perspective, the key goal was to simplify the experience by organizing flows, reducing friction and building a consistent design language, turning complex operations into simple, intuitive interactions.
