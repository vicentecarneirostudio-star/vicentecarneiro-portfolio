Christmas dinner, made easy. That’s ALDI Planner.

The Christmas Dilemma

Hosting Christmas dinner is one of the most stressful moments of the year. What to cook, how much to spend, how many people to feed and what to buy turn a celebration into a long list of decisions, and many people simply don't know where to start. For ALDI, the challenge went beyond sharing recipes. It needed a useful, easy-to-use tool that would engage customers throughout the Christmas campaign, put its products at the center of the menu and bring people to aldi.es, all under the promise of "Así de fácil, así de ALDI".

The Challenge

1. Solve a real problem for ALDI customers.

2. Keep ALDI top of mind on a date as important as Christmas.

3. Improve the KPIs for users visiting our website and app.

Conclusions & Learnings

A planner people used, came back to, and that drove traffic to aldi.es

ALDI Planner turned a seasonal campaign into a tool people actually used. During the Christmas campaign it reached over 60K visits and nearly 50K users, who spent more than a minute per visit building their menus. 15% of them came back within a month, and the planner sent around 5K visits to aldi.es, adding 4% to the website's traffic. These results showed that a useful, easy-to-use experience can bring people back and connect a campaign with the brand's main site, and they pointed to clear next steps: evolving the planner from a holiday host tool into a weekly menu planner, and driving more traffic from it to engagement content.
